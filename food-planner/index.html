Food reference

Typical serving sizes with calories, protein, carbs, fibre, sugar, saturated and unsaturated fat, glycemic index, glycemic load and an inflammation rating. Tap a category or search by name.
Inflammation rates each food by the overall evidence on dietary inflammation (Mediterranean-style eating patterns and dietary-inflammatory-index research): ✓ Anti — fatty fish, olive oil, nuts, berries, leafy and cruciferous vegetables, legumes, whole grains, coffee and tea; ! Pro — refined flour, added sugar, deep-fried food, processed meat, sugary drinks. Foods with no mark are neutral. It is a shorthand for the pattern, not a verdict on one serving: the overall diet, portions and preparation matter far more than any single food, and a "Pro" food now and then is not a problem.
All macros in grams; kcal is calories. Sat fat = saturated fat, the one fat to watch (it raises LDL — flagged red at ≥5 g); total fat itself is left out (it's already in calories). Unsat fat = total fat − saturated (mono + poly, the fats that replace saturated fat well). † marks deep-fried or ultra-processed foods, where that fat comes with heated oil, refined carbs and a lot of calories — don't count it as "good" fat. It isn't a target, so there's no goal or meter for it. GI (glycemic index, how fast): low ≤55 · med 56–69 · high ≥70. GL (glycemic load, GI × net carbs ÷ 100, impact per serving): low ≤10 · med 11–19 · high ≥20; "—" = negligible carb. Rice & potato are split freshly cooked vs cooked-&-chilled — cooling forms resistant starch that lowers GI even after reheating. Approximate values; brand, variety and prep vary.
